Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What's In The Fridge ("the app," "we," "us") helps you find recipe videos based on ingredients you have. This page explains what information is collected when you use the site and how it's used.

Information We Collect

We do not require an account and do not collect personal information such as your name or email address. Ingredients you enter and recipe searches are sent to our server to look up matching videos and are not linked to your identity.

Cookies and Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad personalization settings at:

  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings

Embedded YouTube Videos

Recipe videos are embedded directly from YouTube. When you view a video, YouTube (owned by Google) may set its own cookies and collect data according to its own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-Party Services

Recipe search results and video metadata are retrieved from the YouTube Data API. Ingredient extraction from video descriptions and audio is processed using OpenAI's API. No data submitted for these purposes is used to identify you personally.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site owner.
