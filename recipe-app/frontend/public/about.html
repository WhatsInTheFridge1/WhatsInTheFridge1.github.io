About This App

What's In The Fridge started from a pretty ordinary problem: staring into the fridge with a handful of random ingredients and no idea what to actually make with them. Instead of scrolling through recipe sites hoping something matches, this app flips the search around — you tell it what you have, and it finds real cooking videos that use those ingredients.

It's an independent, one-person project — not run by a company or a media brand. It's built, maintained, and improved by someone who just wanted a faster way to answer "what can I cook with this?"

How It Works

  • Search by ingredient, or browse ingredients by category (fruits, vegetables, proteins, spices, and more).
  • The app searches YouTube for recipe videos and uses AI to check whether each video's description and audio actually cover the ingredients you listed, instead of just matching on the video title.
  • Toggle "Strict Mode" if you want recipes that use most of what you have and little else, or leave it in the default mode for more flexible matches.
  • Once you open a recipe, ingredients you've already told the app you have get checked off automatically, so you can see at a glance what you still need to pick up.

Why It's Free

The app is supported by ads rather than a subscription or paywall, so anyone can use it without signing up or paying. See the Privacy Policy for details on how ads and cookies work on this site.
